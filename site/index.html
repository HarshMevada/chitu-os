build __BUILD__ · wake word “Chitu”

This browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https (the github.io link) to allow USB access.
  1. Plug Chitu in with a USB data cable and click Connect & install.
  2. Pick the port called “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”. No port? Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, try again.
  3. Do not tick “Erase device” — that keeps your saved Wi-Fi and Xiaozhi activation.
  4. After the restart the screen shows chitu/__VERSION__ for a moment: that is the installed version.

First time? Join the Chitu-XXXX Wi-Fi hotspot from your phone, choose your 2.4 GHz network, then enter the 6-digit code shown on the screen at xiaozhi.me.
